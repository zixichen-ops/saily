What changed and why

  1. Week 2 · Opportunity BriefWe assumed the problem was scattered information, so we planned one web hub for everyone.
  2. Week 4 · 5 student interviewsStudents wanted to know what applies to them and what to do next. We moved to a personalized, mobile app built around tasks and deadlines.
  3. Week 5 · Feedback StudioClassmates and the instructor asked for peer Q&A, housing, cars and used items. We added the Market and Forum, each tied to checklists.
  4. Week 6 · FinalRenamed UniHub. Focused on incoming and exchange students, turned school emails into tasks, and added privacy controls.

2. "You need a room for spring. Find one option and what to check before paying."
3. "Ask other students whether a used-car price is fair."
